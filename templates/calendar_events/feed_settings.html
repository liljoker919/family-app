Paste your calendar's "secret address in iCal format" from Google Calendar or Outlook's calendar settings — no sign-in required, and only you can see it.

How do I find this?

Google Calendar

Settings (gear icon) → select your calendar under "Settings for my calendars" → scroll to Integrate calendar → copy Secret address in iCal format.

Outlook

Settings (gear icon) → View all Outlook settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → under "Publish a calendar," pick your calendar, set permissions, then Publish → copy the ICS link.
